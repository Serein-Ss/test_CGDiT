     Experimental determination of the R(5d)-T(3d) hybridization in rare-earth intermetallics

                                         M. A. Laguna-Marco, J. Chaboy, and C. Piquer
                Instituto de Ciencia de Materiales de Aragón, CSIC-Universidad de Zaragoza, 50009 Zaragoza, Spain
                                       共Received 12 November 2007; published 27 March 2008兲
                We present an x-ray magnetic circular dichroism 共XMCD兲 study performed at the R L2 edge in RAl2 and
             RT2 共T = Fe, Co兲 intermetallic compounds. By analyzing the modification of the XMCD spectra as both the rare
             earth and the alloyed element 共Al, Fe, or Co兲 are changed in the compound, an extra contribution, XMCDT, has
             been identified and undoubtedly associated with the R共5d兲-T共3d兲 hybridization in Fe and Co compounds. The
             sign and intensity of this contribution are found to be related to the magnetism of the T neighboring atoms as
             well as to the strength of the T-R interaction. These findings open the possibility of experimentally quantifying
             both the R共4f兲-R共5d兲 and the T共3d兲-R共5d兲 hybridizations, which govern the R-T interaction.
