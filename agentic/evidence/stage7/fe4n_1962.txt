                Mossbauer measurements of Fes~ were made on ferromagnetic Fe4N, which has a face-centered cubic
             arrangement of iron atoms with nitrogen at the body-center position. The hyperfine fields are 345 koe for
             the corner Fe and 215 koe for the three face-center Fe, approximately proportional to their magnetic mo-
             ments, 344a and 244s. The isomer shifts, measured against a stainless steel source, are 0.30 mm/sec for the
             corner Fe and 0.45 mm/sec for the face-center Fe. These values are in line with their proposed electronic
             configurations of 3d 4s and 3d'4s, which are derived on the assumption that the nitrogen at the body-center
             position acts as an electron "donor" to the face-center Fe. The Mossbauer spectra of (Fe4, 4, Ni4, 4)N and
